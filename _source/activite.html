Our Activities

As you wonder and discover, partake in some vital
components of Bhutan’s culture.

Famous Festivals
of Bhutan

Bhutan is overwhelming with cultural activities, no matter when you plan to visit the region. In particular, visit during festivals to enjoy a colorful phase of your vacation. Every month, Bhutan holds at least one cultural festival or celebration.

A Date With
Buddha

Just a 20-minute drive from Pemako Thimphu lies one of Bhutan’s most significant landmarks—the 169-foot Buddha Dordenma statue, adorned with 125,000 miniature Buddhas. Pay a visit to this majestic structure for a captivating experience with the embodiment of journeys and awakenings.

The Star Reader
Of Paringzampa

Enriched with ancient legends, Pangrizampa College of Astrology has stood since beliefs and practices. Situated a short 30-minute drive from Thimphu town, this revered institution is home to over a hundred monks devoted to the study of astrology.
