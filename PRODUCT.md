# Product

> Written from the user's build prompt and the live site (thimphutower.com), without an interview: the user asked for a single uninterrupted run. Lines marked *(inferred)* are assumptions to confirm.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) with `output: 'export'`: a fully static site with no backend, no auth and no CMS. Specified by the user.

## Users

- International leisure travellers planning a Bhutan trip, usually through a tour operator, who want to check the hotel, its rooms and its location before confirming *(inferred)*.
- Tour operators and group organisers (for example the Harvard/MIT trek groups that stayed there) checking rates, capacity and contact details *(inferred from the downloads and gallery)*.
- Regional and domestic guests who arrive by phone or email and want rates quickly.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Thimphu Tower Hotel, a 3-star boutique design hotel open since 2015. It has 28 rooms overlooking Clock Tower Square and Changlimithang stadium in central Thimphu, plus a restaurant. Success means a visitor understands the location, the rooms and the rates, and sends an enquiry by email or phone.

## Positioning

It is the hotel on Clock Tower Square: the rooms and the dining room look straight onto the city's main public square, its clock tower, its festivals and its stadium. No other Thimphu hotel can truthfully claim that view.

## Operating Context

- There is no booking engine. Enquiries go by email (thimphutowers@gmail.com) or phone (+975 2 335 367). The hotel also mentions MakeMyTrip as a partner channel.
- Check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM (stated on the room pages).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Room types and nightly rates: Deluxe Single Nu 4,500, Deluxe Double Nu 5,000, Premium Single Nu 5,500, Premium Double Nu 6,000.
- In-room amenities stated: AC, geyser, TV, Wi-Fi, fridge, telephone, work desk with chair, sofa, large wardrobe, buffet breakfast included.
- Facilities stated (home page list): free Wi-Fi, free breakfast, baggage storage, dry cleaning, bar/lounge, breakfast buffet, fitness gym, airport transportation, concierge, laundry, restaurant, car hire, ironing, air conditioning, room service, in-room safe, flatscreen TV, family rooms, non-smoking rooms.
- Undecided: room occupancy limits (the source figures look like template placeholders), room sizes, the 360° tour (the source says "Coming soon"), and a form backend.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Thimphu Tower Hotel" (also "Hotel Thimphu Tower"). The logo is the word THIMPHU over a gold clock-tower glyph inside concentric arcs, over the word TOWER (`public/images/original/logo.png`).
- Voice from the source: warm and hospitable; "a balance of traditional and modern"; "meaningful comfort and practical luxury".
- Vision: "To provide the finest hospitality experiences in Bhutan, and in doing so, contribute to nation building." Mission pillars: Creating Memorable Experiences, Growing People, Transforming Lives.

## Evidence on Hand

- 58 gallery photos (`public/images/original/gallery/`): the facade and Clock Tower Square, rooms, the dining room with a view of the square, breakfast, festivals seen from the hotel, the team, community events, and snow on the square.
- 12 room photos, the facade photo (`main.jpg`), 4 activity cards.
- 2 named guest quotes: Sanjay Patel, and Adin B (Harvard-Bhutan Trek).
- 2 Harvard/MIT trek group reports (PDF).
- Absent, and must not be fabricated: awards, review scores, guest-count statistics, room sizes, real floor plans, and any staff names beyond what the captions give.

## Product Principles

1. The view is the product. Lead with the square, the clock tower and the windows that look onto them.
2. Truth over polish: every price, amenity and claim traces back to the hotel's own material.
3. The enquiry is always one step away, and it is honest that it is an email or phone request, not an instant booking.
4. Bhutanese and contemporary in balance, never a pastiche.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many visitors browse on mobile over slow connections while travelling, so the site must stay light and work without JavaScript wherever possible.
